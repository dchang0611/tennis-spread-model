What does each factor add?

Incremental factor tests

Lower Brier scores mean more accurate probabilities. A negative Brier change favors the added factors. An interval crossing zero leaves the direction uncertain.

Probability metrics use one predetermined line per match. Paper selections can use a different line and are counted once per match. Gross returns use displayed prices; fills and liquidity are unverified.

How the baseline works

One chronological feature engine serves replay and daily predictions. The three fixed candidates are Elo, Elo with serve/return, and those factors with recent surface margin. Formats have separate fits and calibration; insufficient evidence closes only that format.

Historical publication timestamps are unavailable. Reconstructed dates use next-day availability plus an extra-day sensitivity test. The historical period is development data. The simplest Elo candidate is frozen for prospective paper collection; no model is promoted automatically.

Player and surface history, recent point-stat coverage, source freshness, quote age, event metadata and pre-start timing must pass their gates. Unresolved outcomes stay visible. No profitability claim is made.
