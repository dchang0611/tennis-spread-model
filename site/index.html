One fixed rule, tracked forward

Keep the baseline’s first locked line only when its claimed probability edge is at least 4 and below 8 percentage points. Keep the same matchup, spread, price, and data checks. A later quote or alternate line cannot replace it. Best-of-3 and best-of-5 results stay separate.

Results assume one unit per recorded line, before execution costs. The historical pattern suggested this experiment; historical replay results are not included here.

COMPACT BY DESIGN

How the current model works

01

Project the margin

The current paper baseline uses overall and surface Elo, with surface and event-level context. Serve/return and surface margin remain research factors. Best-of-3 and best-of-5 have separate fits and calibration.

02

Price each spread

Rolling out-of-sample errors create a full range of plausible margins and a cover probability for every Novig alternate line.

03

Remove the market hold

Both sides of each spread are normalized into a paired no-vig probability before the model claims an edge.

04

Apply the safety gates

A play needs sufficient probability edge, expected return, data quality, and uncertainty-adjusted support. Otherwise it is a pass.

MODEL RESEARCH RULES

Safety thresholds

A line must clear a 4% probability edge and 5% expected return. The board publishes no more than one play per match, choosing the strongest uncertainty-adjusted candidate.

MODEL PERFORMANCE

Rolling validation

Validation uses chronological predictions and the selected match format. New paper results are kept separate by model version. Sources refresh on a rolling schedule with no fixed end date; same-day and future results are excluded using UTC dates.

Research tool only. Prices move, edges are uncertain, and no model guarantees profit.
