One concept, one vote

01

Project the margin

Overall and surface strength, serve and return quality, recent surface margin, matchup fit, workload, and context estimate the final game differential.

02

Price each spread

Rolling out-of-sample errors create a full range of plausible margins and a cover probability for every Novig alternate line.

03

Remove the market hold

Both sides of each spread are normalized into a paired no-vig probability before the model claims an edge.

04

Apply the safety gates

A play needs sufficient probability edge, expected return, data quality, and uncertainty-adjusted support. Otherwise it is a pass.
